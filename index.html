Cancellation & refunds

  • ✅Cancel at least 24 hours before your lesson and you’ll get a full refund.
  • ⏰Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the lesson aren’t refunded.
  • 🔁If I can’t make your lesson on the scheduled day, we’ll reschedule it for a time that works for you.
  • 📅Weekly plans: billed monthly for 4 lessons. Cancel anytime from your plan page; your plan runs to the end of the month you’ve paid for, and that month isn’t refunded.
  • 🚗In-person travel: free within 10 driving miles, then $1 per mile, up to 20 miles. The travel fee is added to your online payment, or paid at the lesson if you pay in person.
  • ✉️To cancel, email Boston.drumming@icloud.com with your booking number (for example BD-1205-K7Q2).
  • 💳Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5–10 business days.
